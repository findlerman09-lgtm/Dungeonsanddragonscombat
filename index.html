⚔ Pre-Combat Checklist

Work through these before initiative is called. Click each step to mark it done.
✓
🎲 Roll Initiative (+9)
Roll d20 + 9 (DEX +5, Alert +4). Initiative Swap: immediately after rolling you can swap your result with one willing ally — bump your best striker to the top, or rescue your healer from a low roll.
✓
🎯 Ambush? (+1d10 to Initiative or Stealth)
Spend 1 Superiority Die to add 1d10 to your Initiative or a Stealth check. Best used when the margin matters — don't burn SD for a roll you'd win anyway.
✓
☠ Apply Poison?
Applying in combat costs your first Bonus Action. Apply before initiative if possible. Effect: on hit, DC 17 Con save or 2d8 Poison + Poisoned condition. Lasts 10 rounds. Potent Poison ignores Resistance.
☠ Applied!
✓
🔍 Know Your Enemy on Priority Target
Use on Turn 1 Bonus Action if not used for poison. Learn immunities/resistances/vulns of the biggest threat within 30 ft. Decides: is poison worth it? Which weapon/damage type to use? Restore with 1 SD (no action) if needed.
✓
⚡ Action Surge — Save or Open Hot?
Save for a 6-attack burst round when a priority target needs to die fast, or open with it on Turn 1 if surprise/range gives an advantage window. Don't waste it when you're already winning.
✓
